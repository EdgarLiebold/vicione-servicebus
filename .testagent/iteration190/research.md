# Iteration 190 research

## Scope

Twenty-three current, previously unadmitted saga-repository sources:

- Query/expression resolution: default/loaded query contexts, expression and property-expression
  factories, property values, filter conversion and state-expression visitation.
- Repository runtime: load/query repository context contracts and factories plus load, query and
  facade repository dispatch.
- Factory/policy boundaries: factory-method construction, new-saga policy, consume-context mode,
  instance-factory delegate and removed-instance exception.

The source areas and their owning tests are split into three non-overlapping agent workspaces.
Requirements, final integration, mutations, coverage/CRAP, evidence and publication stay under the
lead agent.
